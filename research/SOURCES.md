**Copyright:** Page's translation is in copyright, so in-game text paraphrases it. Short phrases
(at most a clause) are quoted and attributed to §.

**Extraction gaps:** the plain text lost the first line or lines of §§1, 5, 8, 12, 17, 21, 25, 28,
31, 35, 38 and 42 at page breaks. §1 ("reigning in the kingdom of the flesh in Seville") is known
only through the essay's quotation of Page.

## Provenance tags used in the game

- **MS**: stated in the surviving Liber.
- **MS-ages**: in the planetary-ages section (§24–33) only. The detailed descent (*immissio*) is
  lost.
- **WILLIAM**: known only from William of Auvergne, *De universo* (1231–36), as reported by
  Page and Fanger.
- **RECON**: our reconstruction from the general correspondence tradition, **not** from the Liber.

## William's objections (as reported), used by the Reader from Paris

The beings inhabit deserts, descend, answer magical prayers, lack angelic names, and are sorted
into twelve orders by element and planet. He calls it planetary idolatry and accuses the author of
founding schools of necromancy.
