# Decisions

Ledger of directional calls. Newest last. Each line says who decided.

1. **2026-09-27 (Ted)** Frame is nested: Dee → monk at St Augustine's → the hermit. Hermit is the playable core.
2. **2026-09-27 (Ted)** Lacuna is diegetic: MS material plays as MS; everything past where the MS stops is playable but visibly marked as reconstructed or William-derived.
3. **2026-09-27 (Ted)** Art: Kenney 1-Bit Pack (CC0, downloaded 2026-09-27 with permission) + Unicode glyphs. OCCULTIMGDB/GoetiaRevEng not used in v1 (Goetia seals are mis-mapped per PIPELINE.md, and the Liber's spirits are not Goetic anyway).
4. **2026-09-27 (Ted)** Ethics: all operations are available; a Suspicion track embodies William of Auvergne's reading. High suspicion relabels spirits as demons and leads to arrest.
5. **2026-09-27 (Claude)** Primary text source is Page's appendix 2 translation (plain text at `E:\pdf\magic\medieval magic\plain_text_drafts\Sophie Page Magic in the Cloister…txt`, lines ~5212–5600), not the essay in Downloads. In-game text **paraphrases** Page and quotes only short phrases, since her translation is in copyright.
6. **2026-09-27 (Claude)** The text-extraction dropped the first line(s) of several passages (§1, 5, 8, 12, 17, 21, 25, 28, 31, 35, 38, 42 are partial). §1 is known only via the essay's quotation of Page. Nothing is invented to fill them.
7. **2026-09-27 (Claude)** William appears inside the hermit's world as "the Reader from Paris", an anachronism by design (the monk is imagining the book's future critic). A note in the Codex says so.
8. **2026-09-27 (Claude)** Planetary hours are simplified to 24 equal hours with sunrise at 06:00, in Chaldean order from the day ruler. Retrograde is a per-planet periodic cycle, not an ephemeris.
9. **2026-09-27 (Claude)** Metals, incense and the named Venus herb (myrtle) are RECON, from the general planetary-correspondence tradition. The Liber itself names only the peony, the shoes, the mirror, and "characters".
10. **2026-09-27 (Claude)** Hosting: none yet. Local only. If it ships, it goes to GitHub Pages per workspace policy. It is static, with no base-path issue because every path is relative.
11. **2026-09-28 (Claude)** The monk layer is redated from c.1278 to **c.1315**. Page (L~412) shows CCC 125 was compiled after c.1305–7; 1278 is only Thomas's approximate ordination date.
12. **2026-09-28 (Claude)** Build slice from `docs/RPG_STRUCTURES.md`:
    - A: spirits are identified by behaviour (the roguelike identification game, carried by the Liber's own taxonomy).
    - E: the abbey library works by shelfmark, with books kept around the abbey (Page's St Augustine's).
    - C: the horarium places the monks in the church during the Hours.
    - F: William's suspicion gets a ratcheting floor (after *Crisis: 1914*), and the abbey is exempt.
    - G: each reader gets their own verbs.
    - B: small version only. Prayer gets graded messages, and purity decides whether a delegated spirit is willing.
13. **2026-09-28 (Claude)** Rejected: permadeath, XP levels, and a random overworld (reasons in `docs/RPG_STRUCTURES.md`).
14. **2026-09-28 (Claude)** Borrowed versus copied books follow the customary's *libri de librario* and *libri de acquisicione sua*. A borrowed doctrine works only while the book is held; Copy at the carrel makes it permanent.
